# Founder–Market Fit Worksheet (expanded)

**Source:** The Founder's Field Manual, Chapter 1 — Founder-Market Fit: Why You, Why Now
**Agent use:** When a subscriber is deciding whether to start, questioning their idea, or asking "am I the right person to build this?" Walk them through the four steps interactively, one at a time. Do not let them skip Step 2.

## Step 1 — Name your earned secrets
List 2–3 beliefs about the market that the consensus does not hold. For each, name the source: a job, a customer, a failure, inside information. **Rule:** if you cannot name the source, it is a hunch, not a secret. Park it.

*Agent coaching questions:* Where did you learn this? Who disagrees with you and why? What would the smartest skeptic say? If your secret were published tomorrow, who could exploit it faster than you?

## Step 2 — Inventory your distribution advantages
Write down 20 names — specific people who will take your call in week one because of who you already are. Not LinkedIn connections. People who know you by name, today.

*Scoring rubric:*
- 20+ names, 10+ in the target market → strong distribution advantage
- 10–19 names → workable, but expect a slower cold start
- <10 names → the gap IS the finding. Options: pick a market where you have the 20 names, recruit a co-founder who does, or budget 6–12 months to earn them

## Step 3 — Run the 12-question self-audit
Walk the chapter's audit in writing. Mark each answer green-flag or red-flag. Most founders show more red than they expect — that is the point, not a verdict.

## Step 4 — Place yourself on the Founder Self-Audit Matrix
Quadrants: **Tourist** (low insight, low commitment), **Mercenary** (low insight, high commitment), **Zealot** (high insight, low staying power), **Operator** (high insight, high commitment). Ask which quadrant the *answers* point to, not the one the founder wants.

If not Operator, name the move: a different problem, a co-founder who fills the gap, or a deliberate plan to earn the missing dimension.

## Common failure patterns (agent should watch for)
1. **Retrofitted secrets** — the founder works backward from the idea to a "secret" invented to justify it. Test: did the secret exist before the idea?
2. **Vanity network** — counting famous or senior contacts who won't actually take the call. Test: when did you last speak?
3. **Quadrant flattery** — self-scoring as Operator while the audit says Mercenary. The agent should gently read the answers back.

## Output the agent should produce
A one-page summary: secrets + sources, distribution count, red/green tally, quadrant placement, and the single named move. Offer to revisit in 30 days.
