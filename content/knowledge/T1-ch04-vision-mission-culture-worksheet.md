# Define and Align Worksheet (expanded)

**Source:** The Founder's Field Manual, Chapter 4 — Vision, Mission, and Culture That Compounds
**Agent use:** When a subscriber is forming a founding team, onboarding first hires, drafting a deck's vision slide, or reports misalignment ("my co-founder and I keep pulling in different directions"). Answers must be written, shared, and edited together.

## Step 1 — Vision (3 attempts)
"We envision a world where ______." Big, bold, timeless. Require three different drafts before settling — the first is almost always either too small (a feature) or too vague (world peace).

*Agent tests:* Would this still be true if the current product died? Could a competitor claim the identical sentence? (If yes, sharpen.) Does it describe the *world*, not the company?

## Step 2 — Mission (1 paragraph)
What do we do? Who do we serve? Why does it matter? All three in one paragraph a new hire could repeat after one reading.

## Step 3 — Core values (up to 5)
Gut-check each value: Would we **hire** for it? **Fire** for it? **Promote** for it? Can we spot it in action *this week*? Would the team agree this is us?

*Agent hard rule:* strike any value that fails the fire-for test — a value nobody would be fired for violating is decoration. Also strike universal virtues (integrity, excellence) unless the founder can name a costly decision the value drove.

## Step 4 — Goals (1 long-term, 3 short-term)
Three-year north star: "In three years, we want to ______." Then quarterly OKRs: one objective, three to five key results, numbers attached. Cross-reference: Measure What Matters field notes (per roadmap) for OKR mechanics; T1-ch16 for metric selection.

*Agent check:* key results must be outcomes ("40% of signups activate in week one"), not tasks ("launch onboarding flow").

## Step 5 — Culture check
What does our team believe that others don't? Culture is the set of beliefs that produce behavior when nobody is watching — and it compounds, which means drift compounds too.

## Common failure patterns
1. **Poster values** — values written for the careers page, contradicted by who actually gets promoted. The promotion record *is* the culture.
2. **Vision inflation for investors** — a deck vision the founder doesn't believe; teams smell it within a quarter.
3. **Solo drafting** — the founder writes alone and announces. The exercise's value is the argument it forces between co-founders *now*, not after the first crisis.
4. **Frozen documents** — never revisited. Put a twice-yearly review on the calendar.

## Output the agent should produce
A one-page alignment doc: final vision, mission paragraph, values with hire/fire/promote evidence, the three-year north star, one quarter of OKRs — plus a list of any co-founder disagreements surfaced, since those are the real finding.
